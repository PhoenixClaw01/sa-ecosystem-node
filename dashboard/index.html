Tools

  • dao_overview Treasury balances, a short proposal list, and data-health checks.
  • list_proposals Galaxy proposals with status, dates, and published vote totals.
  • get_proposal_votes Vote count and voting power for one proposal.
  • dao_revenue Monthly DAO revenue by source from Galaxy.
  • dao_treasury_flows Monthly inflows and outflows for the DAO wallets.
  • dao_payments Payment ledger of treasury outflows, each row linking to Solscan.
  • treasury_history Treasury balance over time from the published cache.
  • ecofund_limit Ecosystem Fund limit and refills from PIP-23.
  • treasury_balances Current SOL, ATLAS, and USDC balances of the two DAO wallets.
  • pip_tracker PIP payments and milestones from the Council Tracker.
  • hub_updates Published hub updates, when a public feed is configured.
  • data_health How fresh each source is, and the last error if a read failed.

Public game data

  • list_star_systems Star systems from the public test realm.
  • get_fleet One public fleet, or the fleets on a player profile.
  • profile_key_audit Keys on a player profile, with permissions and risk flags.
  • galaxy_overview Star systems with starbases and an activity summary.
  • list_categories The ecosystem category ids this server publishes.
