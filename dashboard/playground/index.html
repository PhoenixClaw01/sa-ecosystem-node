Signed records

Load the signed hub records from hub_updates, pick one, and check its ed25519 signature in this browser. The page rebuilds the signed bytes, confirms they hash to the record's hash, and refuses small-order and non-canonical keys before it verifies. Turn on tamper to flip one byte and watch the check fail. This checks the author signature only, not the node's allowlist or approval count.
